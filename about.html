Our Story

Serving Customers and Helping Others Build a Life in the Trades

Holden was built by people who believe skilled trades create better homes and stronger communities. Our local team brings determination, honesty, and years of hands-on experience to every call.

Today we are proud to be a trusted home service company for families throughout the Portland metro area and the mid-Willamette Valley. We hold Oregon CCB License #253995 and stand behind our work with clear guarantees.

Whether you need furnace repair, a new air conditioner, plumbing help, or electrical upgrades, our goal is simple: show up on time, do quality work, and treat your home with respect.
