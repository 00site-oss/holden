Serving Customers and Helping Others Make a Life for Themselves in Trades

Founder and CEO, Aaron Gaynor, got his start in the trades after graduating from high school. Though he struggled in the classroom, and has dyslexia, and his high school principal even encouraged him to drop out, at age 23 he earned his contractor's license and started his first business. In 2007 when the financial crisis hit, Aaron's commercial plumbing business declined and he declared bankruptcy.

Not letting the hard times get him down, he began educating himself for future business success. With the help of a small trusted team, including his sister, Roslyn, Aaron borrowed $50 to start his residential plumbing company.
